# First steps after joining

What a member sees right after joining and choosing a folder: the workspace, still empty, with
the folder being read in the background. The assistant zone greets them with a few first
questions over **their** folder; the workspace says, lightly, where things are. Everything here
is shown once: "Don't show this again" removes the welcome and the tips for good.

The frame is a simplified workspace (no dock) with the real zones' look: the folder zone and the
todos on the left, the open-file area in the middle, the assistant on the right. Under 768px the
middle is gone and the zones stack: assistant, folder, todos.

## Suggestions come from file names, not from reading

The folder's listing is there the moment it is chosen; reading the files takes minutes to hours.
So the first questions are built from names alone, and are ready at once:

- the job most files are about ("Dupont — offer.docx", "Dupont — notes.md" → "What's the status
  of the Dupont job?");
- the latest quarter in a file name ("2026-Q3.xlsx" → "Summarise the Q3 figures");
- the latest meeting note ("meeting 2026-10-02.md" → "What did we agree at the meeting on 2 Oct?");
- a second job, open invoices, the todo list — in that order, four at most.

A folder whose names say nothing ("IMG_0042.jpg", "scan.pdf") gets one generic question, "What
is in this folder?". The risk is a suggestion over a file the assistant has not read yet: the
answer then says how far it got ("Looking through the 3 files I've read so far…").

Clicking a suggestion asks it, exactly as if typed. After the first question the welcome is gone;
the next new chat shows the ordinary first-visit questions of the conversations panel.

## Where things are: pointers, a tour, or nothing

Three zones need naming once: **your folder**, **the assistant**, **your notes and todos**.

### A — Pointers in each zone

A small dismissible note at the top of each zone, read in any order or not at all. It does not
block anything, and each one goes away on its own ✕. The cost: three boxes of text on a screen
that is meant to look calm, and they push the zone's content down until dismissed.

<Story of={Stories.Pointers} inline={false} height="720px" />

### B — Three-step tour

One card at a time (Next / Skip), the zone it talks about outlined. It tells the story in order
(folder → assistant → notes) and leaves nothing behind once done. The cost: it is modal in
spirit — most people skip tours on reflex, and then they have seen nothing. On a phone the zone
it outlines may be below the fold.

<Story of={Stories.Tour} inline={false} height="720px" />

### Baseline — nothing

The zones have titles and the assistant's welcome already says what it does. The member was
just told about the folder on the join page. If the empty states are good, tips may be noise.

<Story of={Stories.NoGuide} inline={false} height="720px" />

Leaning: **A**, because each pointer sits where the thing is and costs one click to remove; but
the baseline is the bar it has to beat.

## An empty folder

No files, no questions. The welcome offers to write a first note (the assistant reads it as soon
as it is saved) or to choose another folder; the folder and todos zones say they are empty.

## Open questions

- Pointers, tour, or nothing? Leaning pointers; nothing if the empty states test well.
- Ask now or wait while reading? Leaning ask now: the names are known at once, and the answer
  can say how far reading got.
- Should the conversations panel's first-visit questions come from the same folder-name rules,
  instead of a fixed list? Leaning yes, one source.
- Is "Don't show this again" per browser or per person (synced to their other devices)? Leaning
  per person.
- An empty folder: is "Write a first note" enough, or should Sandclaw offer a starter (a
  "Welcome.md" with a todo)? Leaning the plain note.
- Do suggestions ever refresh (new files, a new quarter), or only on the first visit? Leaning
  first visit only; the conversations panel covers later.
