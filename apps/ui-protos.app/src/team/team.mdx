# Team

The admin's screen for who is in the group: people, their devices, and invites still
waiting. Every change goes through the Sandclaw machine (`listPeople`, `invitePerson`,
`revokeDevice`, `revokePerson`, `setRole`, `setGroupName`); the screen only has to make each
one understandable to someone who has never heard of a key.

The question: one list that shows everything at once, or a list and a details pane?

## A — One list

People in one column, their devices folded under each person, pending invites at the
bottom. Everything on one page and one scroll; with a dozen people the devices stay folded
and the list stays short. Actions sit on every row, so the page is busy.

<Story of={Stories.OneList} inline={false} height="620px" />

## B — Two panes

People on the left; the selected person's role, actions and devices on the right. Pending
invites get their own tab next to People. Calmer, and room for more per person (when they
joined, who invited them), but invites are one click away and easy to forget.

<Story of={Stories.TwoPanes} inline={false} height="560px" />

## What admins can't do, and why it says so

- The only admin can't be made a member: nobody could manage the group afterwards. The
  button is disabled and the reason is written under it, not in a tooltip.
- You can't remove yourself, and you can't remove the device you are using.
- Removing a person or a device asks first and says what happens: access ends at once;
  files and chats already in their browsers stay there but can't reach the Sandclaw machine.

## Members

Members see who is in the group and their roles. No devices, no actions, no invites.

<Story of={Stories.MemberView} inline={false} height="360px" />

## When the Sandclaw machine doesn't answer

Changes need the machine, so every action is disabled with one line saying why. Copying an
existing invite link still works: the link is already made.

<Story of={Stories.MachineOffline} inline={false} height="620px" />

## Open questions

- A or B? A while the group is small, B once it isn't?
- Should members see their own devices, to remove a lost phone without asking an admin?
- Invite role and expiry: choices as buttons (as here) or one "Advanced" fold, with 7 days
  and member as silent defaults?
- When a person's last device is removed, do they stay in the list (and can come back with
  a new device) or are they removed with it?
- Should "Recent" grow into a full activity list (invites accepted, declined, expired,
  people removed), or stay one line about new devices?
