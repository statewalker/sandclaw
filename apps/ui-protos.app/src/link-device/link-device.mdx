# Link a device

One operation serves two cases. **Adding a device**: Claire wants her new phone to be her, not
a new member. **Merging**: Hugo joined from his home laptop with a separate invite as "Hugo B.",
and now wants it to be the same person as Hugo Benali on his work computer.

Either way, one device keeps its identity (the **keeper**) and the other takes it on (the
**mover**). The keeper opens a link, valid 10 minutes; the mover opens it; both screens show
the same 6-digit code; the person confirms on both, and only then does anything move. Each
story shows both screens side by side: click on each device as the person would.

## A — Compare the code, tap Yes on both

The protocol's default. One glance and one tap per device. Its weakness is the careless tap:
someone who taps Yes without looking gets no protection from the code.

<Story of={Stories.AddCompare} inline={false} height="620px" />

## B — Type the code shown on the other device

The keeper types the code the phone shows; the phone still confirms. Typing forces the person
to read the code, so a careless Yes cannot happen on the device that gives its identity away.
It costs six digits on a keyboard, and on a phone keeper it is fiddlier.

<Story of={Stories.AddType} inline={false} height="620px" />

## Merging two names into one

On his home laptop Hugo is "Hugo B.". Before he links, the laptop says what will happen: he
becomes Hugo Benali there too, one person and one name, and his files and chats on the laptop
stay. The code step repeats it in one line, so the last thing he reads before confirming is
which name is kept.

## When it fails

Every failure says that nothing was shared and what to do next. Only the keeper offers
"Start again", because the new link has to come from there.

- **Expired**: the link passed 10 minutes. The story shortens it to 8 seconds.
- **Taken**: a second device opened the link, so it was closed. The keeper is told to start
  again and to open the new link only on their own device.
- **Declined**: someone tapped No, or the codes were different. In the story the phone shows
  `482 139`: tap Yes anyway on both, or type what the phone shows, to see it.
- **Timed out**: the code was not confirmed on both devices in time. The story shortens 2
  minutes to 6 seconds.
- **The other device left**: it closed before finishing.

## Open questions

- A or B? Or A for adding a new device and B for a merge, where more is at stake?
- In B, should the keeper type the code, or the mover? The keeper gives its identity away, but
  the keeper may be the phone.
- Where does "Link a device" live: only under My identity, or also as an option on the invite
  page ("Already in Atelier Morel on another device?") so Hugo never creates "Hugo B."?
- Should a merge be offered to the admin, who sees two similar names, rather than waiting for
  Hugo to notice?
- After "taken", should the admin also be told that someone else opened a link?
- The "Declined" wording covers both a tapped No and a code mismatch. Should a mismatch say
  "The codes were different" on its own, as a stronger warning?
