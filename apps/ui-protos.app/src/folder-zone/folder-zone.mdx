# Folder zone

The left zone: the user's folder, which is the assistant's working context. The words are
"folder" and "file" — never volume, mount or storage.

## Ready

Search narrows the tree to matching files and opens the folders on the way.

<Story of={Stories.Ready} inline={false} height="480px" />

## While the assistant reads the folder — A: a progress bar

"Getting to know your folder" explains a slow first answer without the word indexing.

<Story of={Stories.IndexingFooter} inline={false} height="480px" />

## B: unread files dimmed

Shows *which* files the assistant does not know yet; the count sits in the zone header.

<Story of={Stories.IndexingMarks} inline={false} height="480px" />

## After a restart

Browsers forget access to a local folder when they restart, and only a click can give it back.
The zone says so plainly instead of showing an error.
