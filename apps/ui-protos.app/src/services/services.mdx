# Services

The services the hub opens to the group's browsers, in one list whatever they are. Sandclaw
comes with two: the **LLM API** (LiteLLM), which the assistant uses for its language models,
and the **LiteLLM UI**, where an admin sees the models and spending. The admin adds the rest:
a documents archive on the office server, the printer's status page.

Each service has a **name** (what members see in their menu), an **address in Sandclaw**
(`/paperless`), **where it runs** (`http://192.168.1.20:8000`, the service's own address on the
office network), and **who can use it**: everyone, or admins only. The address it runs at stays
visible but small and grey: the admin needs it when something breaks, not every day.

Health is one word next to the name: **Working**, **Slow** (answers, in over a second),
**Not reachable**, **Off**, or **Testing…** while a test runs. Sandclaw tests a service when it
is added, turned on, or its address changes; *Test again* is always there.

Routes, headers and rewrite rules are not here. They stay in the technical console, behind one
link at the bottom.

## A — Rows that open in place

One list; a row opens to its details and actions, and *Edit* turns the open row into the form.
Adding puts the form at the top of the list. It reads well on a phone and keeps the whole list in
view, but two services cannot be compared side by side, and the list jumps as rows open.

## B — List and detail

The list on the left, the chosen service on the right; on a phone the detail stacks under the
list. Moving between services is one click and nothing jumps. On a phone the detail and the add
form land below the list, so the admin has to scroll to see what the tap did.

## Adding a service

The form checks only when the admin tries to save, then names each mistake in place: a missing
name, an address that does not start with `/`, an address another service already uses
(`/Printer/` and `/printer` are the same), and a "where it runs" that is not a full `http://` or
`https://` address. Saving adds the service and tests it straight away.

## When a service is down, slow or off

A service that does not answer says what to do, in order: check that its machine is on and on
the office network, test again, edit its address if it moved, or turn it off so it leaves
members' menus. A slow one says so without alarm.

<Story of={Stories.Down} inline={false} height="620px" />

The LLM API runs on the hub itself, so its advice is different: restart the hub. Its line also
says what members feel: the assistant cannot answer anyone.

<Story of={Stories.AssistantDown} inline={false} height="640px" />

Turning a service off keeps its settings; members no longer see it and its address answers "not
available".

<Story of={Stories.TurnedOff} inline={false} height="640px" />

## Test

*Test again* shows **Testing…** in the list and in the detail, then the answer and how long it
took.

<Story of={Stories.Testing} inline={false} height="560px" />

## Remove

Removing asks first and says what happens: the service leaves the menus, keeps running on its
own machine, and turning it off is the way to keep its settings. The two built-in services have
no *Remove*: the assistant needs the LLM API, and the LiteLLM UI comes with it. They can be
turned off, and their address in Sandclaw is fixed.

<Story of={Stories.Remove} inline={false} height="640px" />

## Why members never see this

A member never manages services: what they get is the result, a service's name in their menu
that opens it. A service set to "admins only" is simply not there for them. An admin-only screen
for members would have nothing to show but a list of names they already have in the menu.

## Open questions

- A or B? A fits a list of five; B pays off once there are a dozen.
- Can two addresses overlap (`/llm` and `/llm/v1`)? Today only exact duplicates are refused.
- Should Sandclaw test services on its own every few minutes and tell the admin when one goes
  down, or only when the admin opens this list?
- Is "Slow" over one second right for every service, or should it be per service?
- Should turning off the LLM API ask first, since it stops the assistant for everyone?
