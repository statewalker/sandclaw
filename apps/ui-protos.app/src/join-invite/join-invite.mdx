# Join from an invite

The first screen a colleague sees after opening an invite link. It has two jobs: get them
connected in one click, and make them trust that "connected" does not mean "uploaded".

## A — Single card

The shortest path. Privacy is one line under the button.

<Canvas of={Stories.Card} />

While the browser dials the office machine the button becomes the progress indicator:

<Canvas of={Stories.CardConnecting} />

## B — Explain, then join

Three promises before the button: the AI is in the office, files stay in the browser, the
link is encrypted. One extra click buys the explanation that sets Sandclaw apart from a
cloud chatbot.

<Canvas of={Stories.Explainer} />

## When the office machine is offline

The likeliest failure is mundane: the Mac Mini is asleep. The message names that cause and
who can fix it, instead of a network error the colleague cannot act on.

<Canvas of={Stories.CardFailed} />

## Open questions

- Is B's explanation worth showing on every join, or only the first one per browser?
- Should the failure state poll and join automatically once the machine wakes?
