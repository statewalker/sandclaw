# Invitations log

What happened to the invites the group issued: who joined, which links were declined, which
expired unused or were cancelled, which are still waiting. It is a log to read, not a place to
act. Each invite's state comes from its events: **waiting** (not answered, not expired),
**accepted**, **declined**, **expired**, **cancelled**.

Both variants share the same controls. The period (last 7 / **30** / 90 days, or all) keeps an
invite when any of its events falls in it. The search matches the invite's name, the person who
joined, their device, and who created it, ignoring case and accents ("ines" finds "Inès"). The
state chips filter and count what the period and search left.

## A — One row per invite

One row per invite: its name, role, who created it and when, its state, and the outcome in a
sentence ("Hugo Benali joined from Chrome on Windows", "Expired unused on 2 Oct"). A waiting
invite says when it expires. A row opens to its timeline. Answers "what became of the invite I
sent Marc?" at a glance; the order of events across invites is lost.

<Story of={Stories.ByInvite} inline={false} height="620px" />

## B — Event log

One line per event, newest first, grouped by day, like an activity feed. Answers "what happened
this week?"; the current state of an invite has to be pieced together from several lines, and
an invite still waiting shows only as "created".

## Open questions

- A or B? Or A as the default with B as a second tab of the same panel?
- Should a waiting invite be cancellable from the log, or does the log stay read-only and the
  invite list keep the actions?
- An invite a member made for their own device ("Inès — phone"): show it with the others, mark
  it ("for her own device"), or hide it behind a filter?
- Does an admin need to know an invite link was opened but not answered, or is "waiting" enough?
