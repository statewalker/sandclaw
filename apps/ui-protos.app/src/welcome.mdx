# Sandclaw UI prototypes

Design variants for Sandclaw screens, side by side, with the discussion next to them. Entries are
grouped by topic: the workspace, files, notes and todos, chats, agents, indexing and search,
sites, and the mesh (setting up the machine, joining, managing the group). Each entry has a
docs page that argues for and against its variants, and one story per variant or state to
open on its own.

Everything here is built from the same shadcn primitives (`@statewalker/ui.view.shadcn`) and
theme tokens as the shipped app, so what you see is what the app would look like. Use the
toolbar's theme switch to check the dark theme.

## Adding a prototype

1. Create `src/<topic>/<topic>.tsx` with one exported component per variant. Mock data goes
   in `src/mock.ts`.
2. Add `src/<topic>/<topic>.stories.tsx`: one story per variant or state, titled
   `<Area>/<Topic>`, where `<Area>` is one of the groups above (for the mesh,
   `Mesh/<Set up | Join | Manage>/<Topic>`).
3. Add `src/<topic>/<topic>.mdx`: what question the prototype answers, a `<Canvas>` per
   variant with its trade-offs, and the open questions.

Prototypes are throwaway. When a direction wins, it is rebuilt in the real packages; the
prototype is deleted or kept as the record of the alternatives.
