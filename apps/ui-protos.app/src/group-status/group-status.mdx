# Group status

Every question travels from the browser to the group's Sandclaw machine. When the link is
healthy nobody should think about it; when it is not, people must understand that their data is
fine, that the machine is the problem, and who can fix it.

The pill always shows the group's name: "*Atelier Morel* · connected". Words like mesh, relay or
peer never appear.

## A — Pill only

Quiet. When offline, the pill turns red and says so; the explanation is in its tooltip.

<Story of={Stories.Pill} inline={false} height="120px" />

## B — Pill and banner

While offline, a banner under the top bar says what still works (chats and files in this
browser), what waits (new questions), and who can fix it.
